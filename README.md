# Guide, Checklist - AC Shadows
An unofficial guide, checklist, and progress tracker for the Assassin's Creed Shadows game.

An unofficial checklist, progress tracker, and guide for Assassin's Creed Shadows. Travel through Sengoku era Japan as Naoe and Yasuke while you keep track of every quest, collectible, achievement, weapon, outfit, amulet, trinket, and horse across the base game, the Claws of Awaji expansion, and the free story drops.

[![Google Play](google_play.png)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=me.mmagg.acshadowsguide)

## App Features
* Progress tracking for your total completion and for each category
* Search the whole game by name and check items off right from the results
* Sign in to back up your checklist and pick it up on your other devices, or carry on as a guest
* Include or leave out the DLC in your lists and totals
* Show or hide gear and cosmetics that cost Helix Credits
* Secret achievements and story details stay hidden until you turn spoilers on
* Move checked items to the bottom of a list
* Check off or reset whole categories, or reset everything at once
* Light and dark themes

## Guides
In app guides with tips and strategies.

## Checklists
* Main story quests across the Prologue, Act 1, Act 2, Act 3, and the Epilogue, with choice impacts.
* Side quests for all nine regions: Harima, Iga, Izumi Settsu, Kii, Omi, Tamba, Wakasa, Yamashiro, and Yamato.
* Location activities and collectibles by region, including temples, shrines, Kuji-kiri, hidden trails, katas, horse archery, kofuns, kamon crests, kano paintings, valuable objects, and legendary sumi-e paintings.
* Legendary weapons for Naoe and Yasuke: katanas, kusarigamas, tantos, Bo staves, bows, kanabos, long katanas, naginatas, and teppos.
* Legendary outfits: headgear and light armor for Naoe, helmets and heavy armor for Yasuke.
* Legendary amulets, trinkets, and horses.
* Every achievement and trophy, with notes on how to earn them efficiently.
* All Shinbakufu and Onryo targets and their related quests.
* Story Drops and Featured Events as they arrive in the game.
* Claws of Awaji DLC quests, activities, and collectibles.
